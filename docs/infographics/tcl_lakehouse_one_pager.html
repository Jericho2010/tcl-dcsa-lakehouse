Low-Toil Governed Lakehouse Principles Proven Live

1
Workspace–Catalog SDLC Binding

Environments isolate at the Catalog boundary (workspace → tcl_staging → tcl_prod); schemas stay pure business subjects (bronze_dcsa, silver_dcsa, gold_dcsa, governance_dcsa).

2
Reuse Bronze by Default & Quarantine Explicitly

Raw Bronze preserves all payloads (including poison pills) in governed UC Volumes; Silver enforces LinkML expectations and captures rejected records with exact rule violation reasons.

3
80/20 Dynamic PII Masking on Curated Silver

PII annotations in LinkML propagate to ODCS and attach mask_pii_email on Silver eBL tables (ex***@tasman-shipper.example) without breaking upstream Bronze streaming.

4
One-Way Emission + Fail-Closed Drift Checks

CI recompiles LinkML & Apache Ossie on every PR and enforces git diff --exit-code, ensuring docs, contracts, LDP expectations, and BI models never silently diverge.
